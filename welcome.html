Gold Price Drop Notifier is ready

Get a desktop alert when gold gets cheaper in India.

Get started

  1. Pin the extension. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Gold Price Drop Notifier. Its icon then shows the rate in thousands (158K is about ₹1,58,000) and turns green when the rate is below your alert price.
  2. Open it and pick 24K or 22K. You'll see today's rate for that purity.
  3. Set your alert price. Enter the price you'd buy at, in ₹ per 10 grams, and click Set alert.
  4. Allow notifications. Make sure your computer lets Chrome show notifications (System Settings → Notifications on macOS, Settings → System → Notifications on Windows).

About the rates

Rates come from the India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA), India's benchmark. IBJA publishes them around noon and 5–6 PM IST on working days, and the extension checks for them automatically while Chrome is open. On weekends and holidays you'll see the last published rate.

Prices are per 10 grams and include import duty, but not GST or making charges. Switch on Include 3% GST at the bottom of the popup to see prices with GST.

Your privacy

No account and no tracking. Your alert prices stay in this browser, and the only site the extension contacts is ibjarates.com, to read the rate.
